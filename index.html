Do you like the PlayStation?
Do you long for the days of Total 90 and Teamgeist? Do you remember saving your games onto memory cards? If so, you’ll probably like It’s In The Game.

The world’s first FIFA tribute band, playing the greatest songs ever to grace a FIFA soundtrack. From the classics you forgot you knew, to the belters that have been living in the back of your brain since 2003.

Think landfill indie, rock ’n’ roll, and a few surprises — all ripped straight from the glory days of FIFA. To be honest.. you don’t even have to be a football fan. You just have to be a fan of big tunes.

It’s In The Game.
